Tools I've built

Daniel Takeoff

Takeoff & estimating web app I designed

A browser-based takeoff tool for PDF plans that works on a PC or tablet. It's built around the way I actually run a takeoff:

  • Set scale from a preset or calibrate from a known dimension
  • Measure lengths, areas and counts directly on the sheet
  • Product library with unit pricing, labor, waste, markup and tax
  • Measurements price themselves into a live estimate
  • Export the estimate to Excel or print it
  • Per-job checklists; plans stay on the device
